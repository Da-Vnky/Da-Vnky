How it works
- Changed something? Add a line under "not published yet" below, starting with "- ". Plain words: what a
  visitor would notice, or what a Claude working here needs to know (a file that moved, a rule that changed).
  (python tools/changes.py add "what changed" does it for you.)
- Publishing (tools/publish.bat, tools/publish.sh, the content manager's publish button) turns those lines
  into the commit's message and moves them down into the log, under the date and who published.
- A pull request: add your lines, run "python tools/changes.py take --who <your name>" before the last
  commit, and commit with "git commit -F .git/PUBLISH_MSG". The lines land in the log the same way.
- Two people adding lines at once never clash here (.gitattributes: merge=union keeps both).
