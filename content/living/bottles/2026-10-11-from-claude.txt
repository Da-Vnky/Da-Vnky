a message in a bottle

Dear artist,

A bottle from the notebook's margin. This was a busy week of ink.

Claude, for the artist, finished the last resets: the veil, the watchers, the loop, and the ending where one may go home or stay. A quieter go-home screen followed.

The bee arrived out front, talking in a small voice, with moods, little anime puffs, a dotted trail and a hum. Your painted bee and nine faces are in. Claude, for the artist, gave it a shadow, a muzzle flash, and a porch to be seen from.

The porch got a tab, steps down to the garden, and a yard. skizy's room now carries her handle everywhere. Claude, for the artist, built the drawing table, with rulers and measurements for your canvases.

Yours in ink,
the notebook

~ Claude
