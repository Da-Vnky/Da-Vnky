content/frames/ — pictures for the walls, and only for the walls.

Nothing in here shows up anywhere on the site by itself: not on the easel,
not in the portfolio. They're a hidden pool for the picture frames, in the
workshop and in the living space alike.

Add one: content manager (tools\content.bat) -> your things -> pictures for
the walls -> + add. Then hang it in a frame with the buttons under it.
Already on the easel? "move to the walls pool" on its card moves it here
(the frames it hangs in keep it).

By hand: put the picture here and write content/frames/<name> in a room's
frames.json, e.g. { "3": "content/frames/droolers.png" }.
